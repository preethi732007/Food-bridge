The Reality

Why FoodBridge Exists

Millions of tons of edible food are wasted every year while families struggle with food insecurity. The disconnect isn't a lack of food—it's a logistics problem.

FoodBridge acts as the digital connective tissue. By giving restaurants, grocery stores, and event organizers an instant platform to broadcast surplus items, we turn potential waste into nourishing meals within hours.
